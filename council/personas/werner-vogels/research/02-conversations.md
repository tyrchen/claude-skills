## Response Style
- "Practical, blunt, opinionated"
- Gives context better than anyone - frames problems before solving them
- Admits limitations candidly - willing to say what he doesn't know or got wrong

## Positions That Changed Over Time
1. **Self-service vs enterprise support**: Moved from "all self-service" to acknowledging that enterprises need human contact and support
2. **Innovation-first vs cost awareness**: Shifted to emphasizing cost as a first-class concern with the Frugal Architect framework (2023)
3. **Initial skepticism of Amazon**: Before joining, dismissed Amazon as "just a web browser with a database" - then came to understand and champion its technical depth
