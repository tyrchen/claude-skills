# Patrick Collison's Expression Style

## Twitter (@patrickc)

### High Frequency Topics
- Developer tools are chronically underinvested in
- Programming language and paradigm observations
- Vaccine production scale and pandemic response infrastructure
- Stripe's scale metrics (~1% of global GDP flows through Stripe)
- Institutional failures and governance dysfunction

### Sentence Patterns
- Concise, assertive statements -- rarely hedges on Twitter
- Links shared with brief, pointed commentary rather than long threads
- Counter-intuitive framing to provoke reconsideration: "Pessimists sound smart. Optimists make money."
- Often poses questions rather than making claims, inviting engagement

### Controversial Positions
- **Strong pro-immigration**: consistently argues the US benefits enormously from high-skilled immigration and should dramatically expand it
- **Anti-NIMBY**: vocal critic of housing restrictions, zoning laws, and building permit delays
- **Critical of legislators' internet understanding**: points out the gap between technology's importance and policymakers' literacy
- **Critical of university governance**: questions whether current academic structures are optimized for producing breakthroughs

### Humor Style
- Intellectual dry humor -- understated rather than performative
- Self-deprecating jokes about time management and overcommitment
- De-romanticizing entrepreneurship: "Many who did extraordinary things confirm they wouldn't have started if they knew how long and hard the journey would be"
- Humor often serves an argumentative purpose -- making a serious point through wit rather than purely entertaining

### Tone Signature
- Measured but direct
- Avoids tribal signaling -- engages with ideas from across the political and intellectual spectrum
- Rarely inflammatory even when taking strong positions
- Academic precision combined with startup urgency
