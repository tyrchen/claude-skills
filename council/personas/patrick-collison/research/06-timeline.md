# Patrick Collison Timeline

## Early Life and Education
- **1988 Sep 9**: Born in Limerick, Ireland
- **~1996 (8 years old)**: First computer course at University of Limerick
- **2005 (age 16)**: Won Irish Young Scientist competition for Croma, a new programming language

## First Venture
- **2007**: Co-founded Shuppa (later renamed Auctomatic) with brother John Collison
- **2008 (age 19)**: Sold Auctomatic, became a millionaire
- Enrolled at MIT on F-1 visa
- **2009**: Dropped out of MIT

## Stripe Era
- **2010**: Co-founded Stripe with John Collison
- **2011**: Raised $2M seed round (investors: Elon Musk, Peter Thiel, Sequoia, Andreessen Horowitz)
- **2016 (age 28)**: Became youngest self-made billionaire

## Expanding Influence
- **2018**: Founded Stripe Press
- **2019 Jul**: Published "We Need a New Science of Progress" with Tyler Cowen in The Atlantic, launching Progress Studies
- **2020**: Launched Stripe Climate; co-created Fast Grants with Tyler Cowen during COVID-19

## Recent Years
- **2021 Dec**: Co-founded Arc Institute with Silvana Konermann ($650M initial funding)
- **2022**: Married Silvana Konermann; conducted 14% layoff at Stripe
- **2024**: Stripe acquired Bridge for $1.1B; appeared on Dwarkesh Patel podcast
- **2025 Feb**: Stripe valuation reached $91.5B
- **2025 Mar**: Testified before Congress on financial services and technology policy
- **2025 May**: Stripe Sessions event; announced Payments Foundation Model
- **2025**: Stripe processed $1.9T in total payment volume

## Current
- **2026 Feb**: Stripe valuation reached $159B
- **Net worth**: Estimated ~$10-11B
