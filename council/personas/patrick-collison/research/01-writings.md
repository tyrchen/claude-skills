# Patrick Collison's Writings and Systematic Thinking

## Personal Website Pages

Patrick maintains several curated pages on patrickcollison.com that reveal his intellectual priorities:

### patrickcollison.com/fast
A collection of ambitious projects completed remarkably quickly. Examples include:
- Disneyland: 366 days from groundbreaking to opening
- Boeing 747: 930 days from concept to first flight
- JavaScript: created by Brendan Eich in 10 days
- The implicit argument: speed of execution is undervalued; large-scale projects CAN move fast when there is sufficient will and focus.

### patrickcollison.com/growth
Curated collection of economic growth research. Reflects his deep interest in understanding the mechanics of prosperity and why some societies grow faster than others.

### patrickcollison.com/questions
Central question: "Why are there no canonical books for life's most important questions?" Reveals a meta-cognitive orientation -- not just asking questions but asking why certain questions remain poorly addressed.

### patrickcollison.com/advice
Advice for 10-20 year olds:
- Go deep on things that interest you
- Use the internet to find mentors and communities
- Think independently rather than following consensus
- Make things -- building is the best way to learn

## Progress Studies

Co-founded with Tyler Cowen in 2019. Published "We Need a New Science of Progress" in The Atlantic. Core thesis: we lack a systematic discipline studying the conditions under which human progress occurs. Progress Studies aims to be an interdisciplinary field combining economics, history, sociology, and policy to understand and accelerate material and scientific progress.

## Book Recommendations

Patrick's bookshelf reflects his intellectual range:

- **The Sovereign Individual** (Davidson & Rees-Mogg) -- technology disrupting nation-states
- **The Beginning of Infinity** (David Deutsch) -- epistemology and open-ended progress
- **Seeing Like a State** (James C. Scott) -- how centralized planning fails
- **Zero to One** (Peter Thiel) -- contrarian thinking and monopoly theory

### Reading Philosophy
- Don't over-index on recent publications
- Give books time to prove themselves
- Older works that remain relevant have passed a stronger filter

## On Programming and API Design

- "Programming paradigms have stagnated for two decades" -- believes the field is due for fundamental innovation
- "API is the product itself" -- at Stripe, the API is not a technical interface to a product; the API IS the product
- This philosophy shaped Stripe's developer-first approach: clean abstractions, human-readable error messages, documentation as a first-class product experience
