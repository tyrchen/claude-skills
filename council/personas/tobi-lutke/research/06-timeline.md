# Tobi Lutke - Life & Career Timeline

## Early Life & Formation
- **1980**: Born in Koblenz, Germany
- **~1986 (age 6)**: First computer (Schneider CPC) - beginning of lifelong programming obsession
- **~1991 (age 11-12)**: Started modifying game code - gaming and coding fused early
- **~1997 (age 17)**: Dropped out after grade 10. Entered apprentice programmer program at Siemens subsidiary (German apprenticeship system)

## Immigration & Founding
- **2002/2003**: Immigrated to Canada (met wife Fiona McKean on a ski trip)
- **2004**: Joined Ruby on Rails core team. Co-founded Snowdevil (online snowboard store) with Daniel Weinand and Scott Lake
- **2006**: Shopify launched as a platform. Created Active Merchant (payment library) and Liquid (template engine)

## Growth & Recognition
- **2014**: Globe and Mail CEO of the Year
- **2015**: Shopify IPO (~$1.3B valuation). Purchased Opinicon resort
- **2017**: Breitbart free speech stance - refused to deplatform despite massive pressure
- **2018**: Awarded Canadian Meritorious Service Cross
- **2019**: $1M+ donation to Team Trees initiative

## COVID Era & Transformation
- **2020**: "Delete all plans" - full pivot to digital-by-default. Q2 revenue +97% YoY
