# Tobi Lutke - Key Decisions & Turning Points

## 1. Snowdevil to Shopify (2004-2006)
Realized the software powering his snowboard store was more valuable than the snowboards themselves. Pivoted from merchant to platform builder.

## 2. Choosing Ruby on Rails (2004)
Early bet on an unproven framework. Joined the Rails core team, built community, and made Shopify a flagship Rails application. Technical conviction driving business architecture.

## 3. IPO with Dual-Class Structure (2015)
Went public with dual-class voting shares to maintain founder control. Set the stage for the later Founder Share expansion.

## 4. Breitbart Defense (2017)
Refused to deplatform Breitbart despite 10,000+ complaint emails. Chose free speech principle over PR comfort. Framed commerce platform as neutral infrastructure.

## 5. COVID "Delete All Plans" (2020)
Threw out existing roadmap entirely. Declared "digital by default." Scaled to 7,000+ remote employees. Q2 2020 revenue surged 97% YoY.

## 6. First Layoff - 10% (2022)
Publicly said "I got this wrong" - had bet on a permanent ecommerce shift during COVID that did not fully materialize. Took personal responsibility.

## 7. Strategic Reset - "Main Quest" (2023)
Sold logistics division to Flexport. Cut 20% of workforce. Framed as returning to "main quest" (core commerce platform) and abandoning "side quests." Most decisive strategic pruning in company history.

## 8. Meeting Purge (2023)
Deleted 12,000 calendar events across the company, reclaiming an estimated 76,500+ hours. Radical statement about organizational overhead and attention as the scarcest resource.

## 9. AI Mandate (2025)
Required teams to prove AI cannot do a job before approving new hires. Integrated AI usage into performance reviews. Made "reflexive AI usage" a baseline expectation for all employees.

## 10. Founder Share (2022)
Secured 40% voting control permanently through a special share class. Controversial but approved by shareholders. Ensured long-term founder vision cannot be overridden by activist investors.
